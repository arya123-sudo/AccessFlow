How AccessFlow works

1

Detect

Every remote press is logged with a timestamp. Overshoots, direction reversals, backtracks, wrong selections and long hesitations are flagged as difficulty signals.

2

Optimize

The TV interface is a weighted graph — every (screen, focused tile) is a node, every press an edge. Edge costs combine a Fitts's-law movement cost (distance ÷ target size) with your personal error rate; Dijkstra recomputes the cheapest route to your goal.

3

Discover & suggest

AccessFlow ranks destinations by visits × excess cost from your own measured runs and proposes pinning a shortcut to the winner — nothing is pre-chosen. Recovery uses prefix-based goal prediction over the same graph. No LLM; it's counting and graph math, labelled as such.

4

You approve

Nothing ever changes silently. Each adaptation is explained with its evidence and applied only when you tap Approve.

5

Adapt & undo

Applied adaptations are listed with their evidence in the audit panel above — and every one can be undone, reverting the interface and the graph.

Recording your demo video (under 3 minutes)

  3. 1:10 – 1:50 · The adaptation. When AccessFlow suggests the shortcut, read its reasoning, tap Approve, then run Task 2 and land on the before/after table.
  4. 1:50 – 2:40 · Recovery + close. Try bonus Task 3 (play River Run), trigger the recovery card by backing up a few times, and close on the measured result — the same task in two presses, adapted only with the user's approval.
AccessFlow is a Fire TV experience prototype built for the Amazon Build, Ship, Shape Developer Hackathon (Fire TV track). The TV shell is a simulator — but the weighted-graph engine, Dijkstra path-finding, difficulty detection, adaptation logic and all metrics on this page are real, computed live from your interaction. Your learning data (run statistics, learned weights, movement profile) is stored only in this browser on this device and accumulates across visits; adaptations are approved per session and never silently restored. Clear the stored learning any time with Reset learning in the Live metrics panel.
